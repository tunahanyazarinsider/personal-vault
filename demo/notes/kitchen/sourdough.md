# Sourdough schedule

## Starter

Feed 1:5:5 (starter, flour, water) the night before. It is ready when it has doubled and a spoonful floats.

## Dough

- 75% hydration, 2% salt, 20% starter.
- Four folds, 30 minutes apart.
- Bulk until the dough has grown by about half, then shape and rest in the fridge overnight.

## Bake

Dutch oven, 250 C with the lid for 20 minutes, 220 C without for 25.
